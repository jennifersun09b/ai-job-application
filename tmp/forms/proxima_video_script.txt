Proxima video elevator pitch – spoken version, about 1 min 50 s (about 260 words)

And honestly, it felt a lot like a supplier evaluation – first agree what good looks like, then compare the options fairly.

For the graduate programme, what attracts me is the mix of training, like the case study programme, and real client work from the start. I learn best by doing, so this really suits me.
