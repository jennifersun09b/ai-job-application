About Us

Ipsos Jarmany is a data management and analytics solutions provider that helps businesses drive growth by delivering data analytics & AI solutions that matter.

About the Team

Ipsos Data Labs serves as the specialised division for data, analytics, and AI within Ipsos, functioning as a critical partner to businesses seeking to enhance decision-making with speed and confidence.

By harnessing cutting-edge data science, analytics, and AI technologies, we deliver actionable insights and solutions that help to uncover untapped opportunities, enhance efficiency through automation, maximise returns on investments, and better mitigate risks while focusing attention on what matters. We connect diverse data domains into a unified view and leverage AI as a powerful enabler, ensuring the right people have the right information at the right time for faster, more confident decisions.

The team is made up of around 80 data scientists, engineers, and commercial analytics who are highly skilled and technology agnostic, enabling them to harness the right data and technologies in order to address the needs of clients with optimum impact and effectiveness. The sectors we operate in include technology, consumer goods, FMCG, manufacturing, travel and tourism, and public sector. The solutions we provide include data strategy and transformation, data science and AI, data platforms, data visualisation, reporting and management and cloud computing.

About the Role

As a Graduate Data Analyst, you’ll be working on major projects for globally recognised organisations. You’ll work directly with clients and internal Ipsos teams to help harness and analyse data, while receiving continuous support and training from our consultants and support teams.

You’ll have the opportunity to challenge yourself, to fully utilise your capabilities and to gain front-line experience with our clients. Your role will be varied, flexible and you’ll develop a wide range of business analysis and support skills to help you work more effectively.

We take pride in supporting charities and ensuring that we're acting as sustainably as possible. We track our carbon footprint and are making conscious decisions to minimise our impact on the environment, from encouraging our people to use public transport and reducing our commercial waste.

You’ll benefit from:

Competitive salary, starting at £28,000 and rising progressively to £34,500 at 18 months.

Thorough training & onboarding programme.

Hybrid working, with 2 days at home and 3 days based in our St. Katherine Docks office.

Regular company socials.
