Graduate Business Data Analyst Join Isio as a Graduate Business Data Analyst and use data to help improve outcomes for pension schemes and their members. You will work with colleagues across the business to understand client needs, analyse information and support practical solutions. You will learn how to identify where pension scheme data can be improved, document business requirements and contribute to projects and client deliverables. You will also build your understanding of emerging digital technologies and how data supports better decisions. You will be employed on a permanent basis from the start. Your responsibilities will grow as your skills, knowledge and confidence develop. What does the role entail? Prepare, check and analyse pension scheme data Identify data-quality issues and support improvements Help develop and deliver projects and client solutions Gather, document and maintain clear business requirements Work with client-focused teams to understand objectives and deliver agreed outcomes Collaborate with colleagues across different teams and disciplines Present findings clearly to technical and non-technical audiences Build knowledge of data tools, digital developments and the pensions sector What We’re Looking For An interest in data, technology, business and solving problems The ability to analyse information carefully and explain your thinking The ability to organise your work and manage competing priorities effectively Clear written and spoken communication A willingness to learn new tools, methods and technical skills The ability to work collaboratively with colleagues and clients High levels of care taken over the quality and accuracy of your work Constructive responses to feedback Those that stay curious and look for better ways to solve problems Consistently demonstrate respect, fairness and integrity We will assess these behaviours in a structured and consistent way during the recruitment process. Training and development You will receive structured learning, practical experience and support from your team. This includes a graduate induction, role-specific training, opportunities to develop wider market knowledge, a buddy to help you settle in and support from a Performance Manager with your progress and development. Subject to fulfilling eligibility criteria, you will be supported in achieving a Bachelors’ Degree level external qualification in Data Analysis, through a Level 6 Data Scientist Apprenticeship. Length of programme You will be employed on a permanent basis from the start of your graduate role. The qualification in Data Analysis should be completed in three years. Location and working pattern This role is based in our Birmingham office. Expected office attendance, including the first few months is four days per week minimum in office and then two/three days a week in the office moving forwards. The working pattern will reflect client, team and learning needs. Reasonable adjustments will be considered where appropriate. Entry Requirements You must have completed an undergraduate degree or higher-level qualification by 1 September 2027 and achieved a 2.1 or higher. If you are in your final year, you should be on track to achieve this We welcome applications from people studying any subject You will need an interest in data, technology and business problem-solving We are not able to provide visa sponsorship for this position and therefore you will need to be able to work in the UK without the need for visa sponsorship now or in the future.
