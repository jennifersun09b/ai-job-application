Job Description Are you ready to make a meaningful impact through data-driven insights and collaborative innovation? We’re looking for a passionate and purpose-led Graduate Analyst to join our dynamic consulting team. This role offers a unique opportunity to contribute to transformative sourcing and Industrialised Construction programmes that drive efficiency, sustainability, and societal value across industries. What You’ll Do As a key member of our team, you’ll support strategic supply chain initiatives through a blend of analytical expertise and stakeholder engagement. Your work will help clients make informed decisions that improve performance and create long-term value. Your responsibilities will include: Utilise artificial intelligence and common data environments to develop tools and streamline procurement processes Conducting client spend analysis to uncover opportunities for optimisation. Performing supply industry research and supply chain mapping to inform procurement strategy and supply chain capabilities for offsite production Creating detailed contractor/supplier profiles to support procurement planning. Participating in client stakeholder interviews and workshops, fostering collaboration and trust. Supporting the development of supply chain strategies and procurement execution plans. Designing impactful presentation materials to communicate insights and recommendations. Supporting stakeholder engagement across Industrialised Construction programmes, contributing to strategic planning and delivery coordination. Contributing to process optimisation initiatives, with early involvement in the development and application of procurement models. Assisting in the integration and documentation of procurement strategies to enable efficient sourcing, supplier engagement, and delivery planning for offsite manufacture and modular construction across multiple markets. Why This Role Matters This is more than just an analyst position - it’s a chance to be part of something bigger. Your work will help organisations build resilient, ethical, and inclusive supply chains that benefit communities and the planet. UK Immigration Mott MacDonald Ltd. are not currently offering sponsorship to candidates under the Skilled Worker visa route in the UK. This decision is as a consequence of the changes made to the Skilled Worker route by the UK Government in April 2024. Our ethos is to develop, train and retain our graduates and therefore we are looking for a long-term relationship with our graduates. For this reason, we cannot accept applications from candidates who would be working under the Graduate visa route or other time-limited visas, (irrespective of visa duration), due to their temporary nature and the requirement for sponsorship under the Skilled Worker route in future. We continue to welcome applications from candidates who are eligible for alternative immigration routes in the UK, that do not require sponsorship as a Skilled Worker now or in future.
