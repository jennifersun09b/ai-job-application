The Analyst Role: You will be a core part of the project team and have a central role in fulfilling client engagements across a range of project types and industries. As an Analyst you will start by assisting more experienced consultants, but as you develop you will take ownership of your own workstreams. You will own key pieces of analyses, execute research programs, and write sections of reports. As you grow within Plural Strategy, you may have responsibility for interns and leading smaller workstreams. You can also get involved in contributing to our Plural Team, whether it is helping with recruitment, social impact projects or joining our social committee.
