Dear Hiring Manager,

I am writing to apply for the Junior Data Scientist position in Digital \& Technology at Haleon. I am currently completing an MSc in Health Data Science at University College London, where a distinction is expected, after graduating from Imperial College London with a BSc in Biological Sciences. Across my academic and professional experience, I have built applications on top of large language models and then measured whether they actually worked. I am particularly interested in this role because it combines generative AI application development with the evaluation and monitoring side of the work, which is the part I have spent the most time on.

What attracts me most to Haleon is the opportunity to build LLM-based and agentic AI solutions for a consumer health business, where the output reaches people making everyday decisions about their own health. My degree is in health data science and my recent work has been on medical AI, so the domain is one I already understand. I enjoy taking an unclear business problem, identifying what users actually need, and turning it into a workf\kern0pt low that can be tested and improved.

At TikTok Shop UK I am a Data Analyst Intern in Business Planning and Operations. Alongside my analytical work I built an AI agent that automates repetitive steps of the analytics workf\kern0pt low, which has been adopted by my own team and by partner teams. Building it meant understanding an existing workf\kern0pt low in detail, deciding which steps an agent should own and which needed a person, and then supporting colleagues as they began to rely on it. I use Claude Code in my development work and review the code it produces rather than accepting it unchecked.

Before TikTok I worked as a Data Scientist Intern at DS Digital Technology on a medical AI engagement for a pharmaceutical client, and that work maps closely onto this role. I developed a Python application that called six LLM APIs with retrieval and search augmentation, tool use and multi-step agent workf\kern0pt lows, generating and scoring over 2,400 responses. I then built the evaluation layer around it: a 246-question benchmark and a six-dimension rubric with red-line rules, scored through an LLM-as-judge framework, which the client adopted as its acceptance standard. I co-built a medically reviewed knowledge graph of 123 entities and 161 relations for retrieval and ran blinded paired A/B tests to measure whether it improved answer quality, because I did not want to assume that adding retrieval had helped. Improving the system meant prompt engineering and comparing model behaviour across six models, and reporting honestly where they broke down.

My other work covers the machine learning and NLP foundations the role asks for. I led a group project at UCL building a clinical NLP pipeline that parsed radiology reports into structured features, f\kern0pt ine-tuning ClinicalBERT and PubMedBERT-style transformer models in Hugging Face and evaluating them against TF-IDF baselines. My MSc dissertation builds cardiovascular risk models on 458,840 UK Biobank participants, comparing logistic regression against three tree ensembles with attention to calibration rather than headline accuracy, and I delivered it as a deployed Streamlit application so that people could run scenarios without touching the code. An earlier project mined consumer feedback from Reddit, YouTube and Twitter using embeddings, clustering and LLMs to identify user intent.

At Ipsos Strategy3 I analysed consumer and healthcare datasets and presented the results to client stakeholders who wanted the recommendation rather than the method. This experience has strengthened my ability to work with business stakeholders and product teams to def\kern0pt ine what a solution actually needs to do, which the role describes as a key responsibility.

I should be clear about where I am starting from. I have not used LangChain or Foundry, and my cloud experience is with A\kern0pt WS at a basic level rather than Azure, so the orchestration framework and the Azure services would be new to me. My Python, my work with LLMs and transformer models, and my experience designing evaluation pipelines for generative AI systems are all well established, and I learn new frameworks quickly. I would bring Haleon hands-on generative AI and agentic experience, a health data science background, and the habit of checking whether what I built actually worked. I have the right to work in the UK until January 2029 without sponsorship.

Thank you for considering my application. I would welcome the opportunity to discuss how my experience building and evaluating LLM-based applications could support Haleon's generative AI capability.
