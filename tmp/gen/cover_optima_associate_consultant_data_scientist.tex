Dear Hiring Manager,

I am writing to apply for the Associate Consultant - Data Scientist position at Optima Partners. I am currently completing an MSc in Health Data Science at University College London, where a distinction is expected, after graduating from Imperial College London with a 2:1 in Biological Sciences, and I work as a Data Analyst Intern at TikTok Shop UK. Across my academic and professional experience I have applied machine learning, statistical modelling and experimentation to problems that someone then had to make a decision about. I am particularly interested in this role because it combines that technical work with client-facing consulting across a range of commercial sectors.

What attracts me most to Optima Partners is the opportunity to work on varied client problems in a practitioner-led team, rather than on one product in one domain. I enjoy taking an unclear business problem, identifying what users actually need, and turning it into a workf\kern0pt low that can be tested and improved. The description of supporting experimentation, model validation and performance monitoring so that analytical outputs deliver measurable impact is the part of the work I care most about, because a model that is never checked against the decision it was built for is not much use to a client.

At TikTok Shop UK I explore large and messy merchant and operations data in Python and SQL, test hypotheses about what is driving performance, and build KPI frameworks and dashboards that operations teams use to set targets. I present f\kern0pt indings and recommendations to product, operations and commercial stakeholders who want the conclusion rather than the method. I also built an AI agent, using Claude Code, that automates repetitive steps of my analytics workf\kern0pt low, and my team and adjacent teams now use it.

At DS Digital Technology I worked on a medical AI engagement for a pharmaceutical client. I designed a 246-question benchmark and a six-dimension safety rubric with red-line rules, agreed with subject-matter experts, which became the client's acceptance standard for evaluating patient-facing AI answers. I then built a Python pipeline calling six LLM APIs with retrieval and search augmentation, which produced and scored more than 2,400 responses through an LLM-as-judge framework. I co-built a medically reviewed knowledge graph of 123 entities and 161 relations and designed blinded paired A/B tests to measure whether it improved answer quality, rather than assuming that it did. That project is where I learned the practical side of applying large language models, including where they do not help.

My MSc dissertation is where the statistics and experimental design sit. I compared logistic regression against three tree ensembles for cardiovascular risk prediction on 458,840 UK Biobank participants, with attention to calibration rather than headline accuracy, then used doubly robust causal forests to estimate the ef\kern0pt fect of lifestyle change at an individual level. I quantif\kern0pt ied how far the conclusions could be pushed before they broke, using overlap weighting, sensitivity analysis including E-values, and external validation on a second cohort, and I reported null results where I found them. I delivered the work as a Streamlit application so that people could run scenarios without touching the code.

My time at Ipsos Strategy3 is the closest thing I have to consulting experience. I structured and analysed data from more than 80 expert interviews, built market-sizing and forecasting models across emerging health-product segments, and turned the results into dashboards and recommendations for client stakeholders. This experience has strengthened my ability to explain an analytical decision to an audience that does not want the technical detail, and to work to a client's timetable rather than my own.

I should be clear about where I am starting from. My commercial experience is at internship level rather than in a full-time consulting role, and my production engineering experience is limited, so operationalising data science solutions is the area where I would need support and where I would most like to develop. My statistics, my Python and R, and my ability to take an ambiguous question through to a defensible answer are well established. I would bring Optima an analyst who is comfortable with experimental design and model validation and who is used to presenting to stakeholders outside the technical team.

I have the right to work in the UK until January 2029 without sponsorship. I am currently based in London and am willing to relocate to Edinburgh for this role.

Thank you for considering my application. I would welcome the opportunity to discuss how my background in statistics, machine learning and experimentation could support your client work.
