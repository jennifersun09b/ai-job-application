Dear Hiring Manager,

I am writing to apply for the Data Scientist I position at QuantumBlack, AI by McKinsey in London. I am currently completing an MSc in Health Data Science at University College London, where a distinction is expected, after graduating from Imperial College London, and I work as a Data Analyst Intern at TikTok Shop UK. Across my academic and professional experience I have taken business and clinical problems, turned them into analytical challenges, built models to solve them, and then argued about whether the metrics used to judge those models were the right ones. I am particularly interested in this role because it combines that work with client delivery across a range of industries.

What attracts me most to QuantumBlack is the opportunity to work on interdisciplinary teams applying machine learning to real client problems rather than to a single product. I enjoy taking an unclear business problem, identifying what users actually need, and turning it into a workf\kern0pt low that can be tested and improved. The emphasis in the role description on translating business problems into analytical challenges and ensuring models are evaluated with relevant metrics is the part of data science I f\kern0pt ind most interesting, because choosing the metric is usually where the real disagreement is.

At TikTok Shop UK I work with large and messy merchant and operations datasets in Python and SQL, applying statistical and machine learning methods to explain what is driving performance across the UK marketplace. I build KPI frameworks and dashboards that operations teams use to set targets, and I present f\kern0pt indings to product, operations and commercial colleagues who do not share my technical background. I also built an AI agent, using Claude Code, that automates repetitive steps of my analytics workf\kern0pt low, and my team and adjacent teams now use it.

On generative AI and agentic systems, which the role lists as a strong plus, my most relevant work is at DS Digital Technology on a medical AI engagement for a pharmaceutical client. I designed a 246-question benchmark and a six-dimension safety rubric with red-line rules, agreed with subject-matter experts, which became the client's acceptance standard for evaluating patient-facing AI answers. I then built a Python pipeline calling six LLM APIs with retrieval and search augmentation, which produced and scored more than 2,400 responses through an LLM-as-judge framework. I co-built a medically reviewed knowledge graph of 123 entities and 161 relations and designed blinded paired A/B tests to measure whether it improved answer quality, rather than assuming that it did.

My MSc dissertation is the clearest example of owning model development end to end. I compared logistic regression against three tree ensembles for cardiovascular risk prediction on 458,840 UK Biobank participants, judging them on calibration rather than headline accuracy, then used doubly robust causal forests on a longitudinal subset to estimate the ef\kern0pt fect of lifestyle change at an individual level. I tested how far the conclusions could be pushed before they broke, using overlap weighting, sensitivity analysis including E-values, and external validation on a second cohort, and I reported null results where I found them. I delivered the work as a Streamlit application so that people could run scenarios without touching the code.

My time at Ipsos Strategy3 is where I learned to work to a client's timetable. I structured and analysed data from more than 80 expert interviews, built market-sizing and forecasting models, and turned the results into dashboards and recommendations for client stakeholders. This experience has strengthened my ability to explain an analytical decision to an audience that wants the conclusion rather than the method.

I should be clear about the gaps. Of the technologies you list, I use Python, SQL, Docker and AWS at a basic level, but I have not worked with PySpark, Airf\kern0pt low, Databricks, Kedro or Kubernetes, and my production engineering experience is limited, so the engineering side of delivery is where I would need support early on. My modelling, my statistics and my experience evaluating generative AI systems are well established. I am happy to travel as the role requires.

I have the right to work in the UK until January 2029 without sponsorship, and I am based in London.

Thank you for considering my application. I would welcome the opportunity to discuss how my background in machine learning, model evaluation and generative AI could support your client work.
