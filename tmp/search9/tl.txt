Introducing Machine Learning & AI at Trainline 👋 Machine learning and AI are at the core of how Trainline is transforming travel, helping millions of customers make smarter, more sustainable journeys every day. Our ML models and AI solutions power critical aspects of our platform, including advanced search and recommendations across our mobile and web applications, pricing and routing optimisation, personalised experiences enhanced by agentic AI, data-driven marketing systems, and AI agents that improve customer support. Our machine learning teams own the complete delivery lifecycle from ideation to production, working closely with stakeholders across the business to expand the understanding and impact of machine learning and AI throughout Trainline. We are looking for a Junior Machine Learning Engineer to join a high-performing, deeply technical community of Machine Learning Engineers, Data Scientists and Data Engineers who tackle complex problems by combining Trainline's rich datasets with cutting-edge algorithms.
