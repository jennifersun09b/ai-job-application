A bit about the role: The Argos Analytics team is undergoing significant transformation, leveraging data to drive change. This analyst role in Argos Commercial Analytics focuses on commercial strategy, including evaluating price campaigns and identifying key steps for pricing strategy development. We want you to have a point of view, combining commercial insight, curiosity, and an understanding of our business to provide recommendations and identify opportunities for improvement. As an Associate Analyst at Argos, you'll play a key role in unlocking the value of our data to drive commercial success. Working within a high-performing team of Analysts and supported by Analytics Managers, you’ll help shape and deliver Argos’s strategic ambitions through data-driven insight. Deliver Commercial Value: Focus on generating analytical insights that support our Commercial business, helping inform pricing strategies and business decisions. Apply Analytical Thinking in Context: Use your strong analytical foundation to understand how Argos operates, recommending tailored approaches to data analysis that reflect our unique business model. Collaborate with Stakeholders: Build and maintain strong relationships across the business, championing a collaborative approach to requirements gathering and analysis development. Stay Purpose-Driven: Work at pace while maintaining high standards of accuracy and professionalism. Communicate with Impact: Present findings with empathy and clarity, translating complex data into compelling stories that resonate with non-technical audiences and inspire action. What you need to know and show: Analytical Mindset & Problem Solving: Apply logical, creative thinking to complex commercial challenges.
