The team supports a variety of digital products, among which are the Tesco Groceries & Clubcard app, self-checkout tills in stores, and the Search and Recommenders APIs. Everything is underpinned by our continuous drive for the best tools and technology to deliver our vision. You will be responsible for This role is within the Online Customer Experience Product Analytics team. You’ll partner with CX product teams across the Online shopping space – primarily focused on delivering improvements to the end-to-end digital experiences for a range of customer journeys and propositions. You could be working on analytics of the digital experience of what customers should see entering our site or app, improving search and browse shopping experiences to help customers find the right products, or even enabling customers to finish their shop through the most effortless and simple methods which work for them. Whilst specific responsibilities will be dependent upon the changing needs of the Tesco business, the following provides an overview of the role’s key responsibilities: • Build relationships and work closely with Product Managers, UX, business teams, and engineering. Help them prioritise, analyse product releases, and recommend opportunities in line with the product vision and roadmaps • Define product level objectives & key results, set and report on targets.  • Design analytics tracking, health and success metrics. Improve data quality and coverage in collaboration with engineering and product teams. • Take defined problems and identify data driven approach to validate and solve them • Deliver analytics on the digital customer journey: gather and shape analytics requirements, define success metrics, validate hypotheses and assumptions, identify and validate sources of data, analyse customer behaviour, deliver insights and recommend opportunities to improve the product along its objectives. • Formulate and communicate results using high quality textual, numeric, and graphical methods appropriate to the target audience. • Build and automate reporting on customer behaviour, key results, and KPIs • Follow our Business Code of Conduct and always acting with integrity and due diligence.
