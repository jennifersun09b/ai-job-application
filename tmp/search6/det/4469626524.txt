How You Will Energise Our Team 🤝 At Valda Energy, we take pride in delivering the highest standards of customer service. As a Graduate AI Performance Analyst, you’ll be responsible for the end-to-end ownership, performance and continuous improvement of our Virtual Assistant, delivered through the Cognigy platform. This is a newly created role, giving you the opportunity to make it your own and play a key part in developing our AI and customer experience. Alongside leading the Virtual Assistant, you’ll deliver operational insight, contact centre forecasting and management information to support informed business decisions. You’ll use customer demand and operational data to understand performance, plan future resource requirements and identify opportunities to improve customer experience and operational efficiency. Working closely with the Contact Centre Manager, you’ll use operational insight, customer behaviour, AI performance data and contact centre analytics to identify improvement opportunities, develop new use cases and optimise customer journeys. We’re open to different levels of experience, from graduates with relevant placement or internship experience to those with experience in a data-driven or operational environment. What matters most is your analytical ability, curiosity and enthusiasm for using data and technology to make a difference. Your day-to-day responsibilities will include but not be limited to the following: Owning the performance, governance and continuous improvement of the Virtual Assistant within the Cognigy platform,
