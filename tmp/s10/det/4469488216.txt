Job Summary JOB DESCRIPTION Are you fascinated by high-paced algorithmic trading, data science and complex engineering systems? Improve and expand existing tooling for data analysis and trend detection as new needs and use cases arise, leveraging AI and our automation frameworks. Research how liquidity and market structure evolve across European markets, and turn that into thought-leadership content clients can rely on.
