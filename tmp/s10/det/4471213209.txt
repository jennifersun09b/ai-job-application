About The Role The Associate Data Scientist supports the development and delivery of data-driven solutions that enable better decision-making across scientific, digital, commercial and operational functions. Working closely with data engineers, life scientists and other business stakeholders, you will apply machine learning to solve real-world challenges. This position reports to the Principal Data Scientist and is part of the Data & AI Team located in Cambridge. It is an on-site role. In This Role, You Will Have The Opportunity To Explore, clean, and transform complex datasets from multiple sources. Apply statistical and machine learning techniques to solve business problems and improve operational performance. Collaborate with stakeholders to understand business requirements and translate them into well-defined problems. Communicate findings through clear visualisations, presentations, and written recommendations. Support the deployment, monitoring, and continuous improvement of data science solutions. The Essential Requirements Of The Job Include A Bachelor's or Master's degree in a quantitative subject. Proficiency in Python. Familiarity with machine learning concepts, including supervised and unsupervised learning techniques. Very strong analytical, problem-solving, and critical-thinking skills. Excellent communication skills with the ability to explain technical concepts to non-technical audiences. It would be a plus if you also possess one or more of the following: Experience with cloud-based computing environments, especially AWS. Experience with software development according to CI/CD principles, including containerisation. Understanding of causal inference methods, e.g. DAGs.
