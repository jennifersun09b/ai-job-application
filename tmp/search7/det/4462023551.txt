The role As a Business Intelligence Analyst, you’ll play a key role in shaping how we use data to drive growth and business impact. You’ll translate complex datasets into strategic insights and actionable recommendations that move the business forward. This is a hands-on role where you’ll collaborate with cross-functional teams across Commercial, Product and Operations to ensure stakeholders have the right data, insights and metrics to make confident decisions. You’ll own analytics projects end-to-end, from understanding the initial business problem through to extracting and analysing data, developing visualisations and communicating meaningful recommendations. How To Make An Impact Lead end-to-end analytics projects, from scoping stakeholder requirements to data extraction, analysis in SQL, visualisation and presentation of actionable insights. Partner closely with stakeholders across Commercial, Product and Operations to identify strategic opportunities for growth and improvement. Develop and maintain dashboards and analytical frameworks that enable teams to make informed, data-driven decisions. Help define and maintain consistent business metrics and KPIs. Translate complex data into clear, compelling narratives and recommendations that drive measurable business outcomes. Challenge assumptions and requirements to ensure we're solving the right business problems. Continuously monitor and improve data quality, ensuring stakeholders can trust the insights they use. Proactively explore data to uncover emerging trends, risks and opportunities. Your background A degree in Computer Science, Engineering, Mathematics, Statistics, Economics or another quantitative field, or equivalent professional experience. 2+ years of professional experience in Business Intelligence, Data Analytics or a similar analytics role. Advanced SQL skills, with the ability to independently extract, manipulate and analyse complex datasets. Experience using Tableau or another mainstream BI/data visualisation tool, such as Power BI, Looker, Metabase or Sisense. Strong commercial awareness and an ability to connect analysis with meaningful business outcomes. Excellent communication and stakeholder management skills. A proactive approach, with the confidence to ask questions, challenge assumptions and take ownership. Comfortable working with ambiguity and turning complex business requirements into meaningful insights. The role is based in London, UK, with a hybrid working model requiring three days per week in the office.
