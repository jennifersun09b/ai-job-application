Own end-to-end machine learning lifecycle from problem formulation and data exploration to model training, evaluation, deployment, monitoring, and iteration in production. Collaborate closely with product managers, software engineers, and data teams across Europe and China to align AI capabilities with business goals and regional market needs. Work onsite in Green Park, London. Strong programming skills in Python, with proficiency in machine learning frameworks such as PyTorch, TensorFlow, or JAX. Solid understanding of modern machine learning techniques including deep learning, NLP, recommendation systems, or LLMs. Experience with cloud platforms (AWS, GCP, or Azure) and containerisation (Docker, Kubernetes).
