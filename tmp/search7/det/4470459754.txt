Your impact This is an exciting opportunity to join our Data & Digital team as a Data Analyst, helping organisations turn data into meaningful insights that drive operational and strategic decision-making. Day to Day Gather, cleanse, validate and transform data from multiple internal and external sources to support reporting and analysis requirements Create, develop and maintain reports, dashboards and visualisations using Power BI, Tableau or equivalent reporting tools Apply analytical techniques to identify trends, patterns, anomalies and opportunities for improvement. Produce regular and ad hoc analysis to answer business questions and support decision-making Write efficient SQL queries to extract, manipulate, validate and analyse data from structured and semi-structured sources Support the creation and maintenance of performance measures, KPIs and management information reporting Document analytical methodologies, assumptions, calculations, definitions and data sources Present findings and recommendations clearly to technical and non-technical stakeholders Support data quality, governance and information management activities to ensure trusted and reliable reporting Work collaboratively within agile, multidisciplinary delivery teams to deliver analytical outputs Assist in identifying opportunities to improve reporting, processes and analytical capabilities Communicate progress, risks and insights effectively with project teams, stakeholders and clients What's In It For You Work on meaningful data and analytics projects that support critical services and organisations across the UK Develop expertise in data analysis, reporting, visualisation and business intelligence tools Gain hands-on experience working with large and complex datasets across diverse client environments Collaborate with experienced analysts, engineers, architects and business stakeholders Build your technical capabilities across SQL, Power BI, Tableau and modern analytics practices Access mentoring, knowledge sharing and professional development opportunities to support your career growth.
