Role Overview We are seeking a skilled and motivated Data Scientist I to join our team. Collaboration & Communication: - Participate in design meetings to help shape the solutions that the team delivers - Present findings and recommendations to technical and non-technical audiences. - Acquire domain knowledge to inform modelling opportunities and model feature creation Qualifications Education: - Bachelor’s degree in data science, Statistics, Mathematics, or a related field.
