The Opportunity Our Data & AI team helps clients turn data into better decisions, improved services and measurable outcomes. We combine consulting, analytical and technical expertise to help organisations understand their challenges, identify opportunities, and deliver practical solutions. Working across a range of sectors, we support clients with data strategy, analytics, insight generation, digital transformation and the responsible adoption of emerging technologies, helping them realise greater value from their data and information assets. Support the delivery of data, analytics and AI projects, helping clients solve business and operational challenges through the effective use of data. Analyse and interpret data from a range of sources to identify trends, generate insights and support evidence-based decision making. Technical skills: Understanding of data analysis, statistics or quantitative methods; Familiarity with data visualisation and reporting; Awareness of data management, governance and information management principles; Exposure to programming, scripting or analytical tools; Understanding of databases and data structures; Interest in AI, machine learning or advanced analytics.
