Job Description Kick-start your career in insurance in a role where your analytical skills will help inform commercial decisions and support real business performance. As a Graduate Trading Analyst in our Broker Trading team, you’ll support delivery of the trading plan across our Motor, Van and Home insurance portfolios. You’ll turn data into meaningful insight across the sales funnel, help monitor trading performance and explore competitor and market trends. Your analysis will support decisions around acquisition, retention and customer value, while helping ensure key trading metrics are accurate, timely and clearly explained. This is an opportunity to build confidence in a fast-paced trading environment, combining numerical curiosity with clear communication and commercial thinking. You’ll work collaboratively with colleagues, learn how insurance portfolios perform and develop your ability to translate analysis into useful recommendations. Key Responsibilities Support trading activity across the Broker Insurance portfolio to help optimise customer acquisition, retention and value. Analyse quotes, conversion, sales and value metrics to provide clear insight into performance across the sales funnel. Explore competitor activity and market trends to help identify opportunities and inform future trading scenarios. Prepare, validate and maintain trading data so analysis is accurate and data quality and governance standards are maintained. Provide analysis for business case appraisals, helping colleagues make informed commercial decisions. Deliver accurate, timely analysis and reporting that makes key Broker performance metrics clear and usable. Support external reporting requirements with reliable analysis and management information when required. Qualifications You’ll Bring A 2:1 degree or above in Mathematics, Statistics, Economics, Actuarial Science, Data Science, Business Analytics, Engineering, Physics, Computer Science or another numerate discipline with a strong analytical focus. Strong numerical and analytical capability, with evidence of applying data analysis during your degree. The ability to interpret complex information and communicate findings clearly. A commercially focused, action-oriented approach to using analysis to support business performance. Strong communication skills and a collaborative approach to working with others. Curiosity, organisation and a willingness to learn in a fast-paced trading environment. Nice to Have Knowledge or experience of Databricks, SQL, SAS or Power BI. Experience or knowledge of Motor, Van or Home insurance, including portfolio economics, customer value or retention drivers. Programming skills, for example VB or Java.
