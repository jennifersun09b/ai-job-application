We’re looking for an innovative and detail-driven Data Visualisation Analyst to manage, design, and bring to life the data outputs for our global Events business. This is the perfect entry-level opportunity if you’re looking to launch a career in data analysis, design, and reporting. Every year, Citywire hosts over 100 events around the world - from roundtable debates in Miami and forums in Paris to multi-day conferences in Bangkok and Cape Town. Each event generates valuable delegate feedback and performance data that needs to be collated, consolidated, and presented to our clients. Your reports will play a central role in delivering value to our clients, helping our sales teams demonstrate impact, and uncovering key industry trends for internal and external stakeholders. What you'll be doing: Overseeing the end-to-end flow of event data from our in-house app directly into client-facing reports. Designing clear, engaging performance reports that support the sales team and highlight event ROI for key clients. Finding creative, fresh, and impactful ways to present and promote Citywire’s event data using design and data visualisation tools. Helping analyse event data to spot emerging industry trends and turn raw numbers into actionable insights. What we're looking for: Analytical Mindset: Strong data analysis skills with an ability to identify patterns, trends, and key insights. Design Talent: A keen eye for layout, typography, and visual presentation. Tech & Design Tools: Proficiency in Photoshop, InDesign, PowerPoint, and Flourish (or a strong desire to master them quickly). Web Basics: Basic HTML skills to assist with digital presentation outputs. Communication: Ability to work collaboratively with sales, events, and client-facing teams.
