Data Scientist - Graduate (12 Months) (Problem Solving & Industrial Insight)
PurpleSector · Thursley (Surrey) (Hybrid)
Deadline: November 23rd, 2026
Salary: £30,000 + benefits
Degree required: Bachelor's
Who are PurpleSector?

Born in the white heat of Formula 1, PurpleSector is an engineering-led, data-driven consultancy that solves complex, high-value challenges.

With a team of over 60 engineers and data scientists, we use race-derived tools, technologies and techniques to uncover critical insights, prototype and validate solutions, and embed lasting performance improvements. Our mission is to decode complexity, unlock efficiency, and deliver measurable results at race pace - turning challenges into performance gains in weeks, not months.

Role Overview

We are looking for an analytical, detail-focused problem solver to help PurpleSector turn complex operational information into clear, useful insight. You will work with engineers, data scientists and client teams to investigate questions, identify patterns, check evidence and communicate what matters.

Practical problem solving and sound judgement over deep theoretical data science

Careful review of information, with a strong eye for detail and consistency

Clear, concise outputs that help teams make decisions and improve performance

A Formula 1 mindset: curiosity, precision, pace and continuous improvement

This role suits someone who enjoys getting to the root of a problem, is not afraid to ask good questions, and takes pride in producing accurate, useful work.

Location: Hybrid (ability to work at our Head Office in Thursley, Surrey and travel to client sites when required).

Key Responsibilities:

Break down ambiguous questions into clear steps, evidence and outputs

Review and organise information from multiple sources, checking completeness, consistency and context

Compare information, identify patterns or anomalies, and follow up on anything that does not make sense

Apply logical, structured approaches to classify, prioritise and summarise findings

Prepare clear, concise reports, tables, charts and presentations for technical and non-technical audiences

Work with engineers and client teams to understand processes, test assumptions and validate conclusions

Use spreadsheets, Python or other practical tools to support analysis and repeatable workflows, with training provided where needed

Document methods, decisions and results so that work can be reviewed, reused and improved

Contribute to rapid prototypes and practical tools that make analysis faster, clearer or more reliable

Communicate issues early, manage priorities and take ownership from question through to answer

General Requirements:

Recently completed a STEM degree such as engineering, physics, mathematics, computer science, natural sciences or a related subject

A strong problem-solving mindset and genuine curiosity about how things work

Excellent attention to detail and a disciplined approach to checking work

Comfortable working with structured and unstructured information, and learning unfamiliar tools quickly

Able to explain reasoning clearly in writing and conversation

Organised, reliable and able to manage several tasks with changing priorities

Confident asking questions, challenging assumptions constructively and working collaboratively

Practical experience with Excel, Python or similar tools is useful but not essential; advanced data science experience is not required

Flexible to travel to client sites in the UK and Europe; an interest in motorsport is desirable

Life insurance x 4 of salary

Income Protection (after 6 month probation)

Employee Assistance Scheme
