Data & AI Engineer We have an exciting opportunity for a bright, passionate, and driven individual to join our successful, dynamic, and reputable multi-utilities business. This role will sit within our Technology team and requires an individual with a high-level of competency in data analytics, automation and AI and a proactive approach to problem solving and the confidence to independently learn new technologies. This is a junior role within the team and would suit a recent graduate in Computer Science or similar. This role will be dedicated to identifying opportunities and improvements to enhance our current systems and their ability both through the use data analytics and artificial intelligence (AI) solutions to identify opportunities, realise efficiency savings, minimise risks and improve customer service. This role is based onsite full time in our Leatherhead, Surrey office. Key Accountabilities The Data & AI Engineer is accountable for using software, artificial intelligence and machine learning to analyse and interpret large amounts of data and support the successful implementation of new technologies and improved system functionality, including automation across ESP. Review and understand business processes and data requirements and specify data analytics and automation solutions Produce simple to understand management insight using Microsoft Fabric, Power BI and machine learning to analyse large amounts of data to support improved decision making, Implement process automation solutions using Microsoft Copilot Studio, Power platform (and other AI solutions and relevant scripting languages) to help the business to improve business efficiency and customer experience Maintain and support data platform to ensure data security, integrity and accuracy in all reports and analytics. Maintain and support automation solutions to ensure continued efficient operation, and business process continuity. Continuously improve and optimise existing reports and automation processes. You will be an advocate for Data Analytics and AI within the business and will take a proactive role in ensuring that individuals and teams have the systems and resources in place that will enable ESP to deliver in line with our fast-paced and agile commercial strategy. This individual will produce simple to understand management insight by using technologies including artificial intelligence (AI), to analyse large amounts of data to help improve decision making.
