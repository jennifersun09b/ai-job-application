## Why Altrad failed

## New freshness rules (applied below)
1. LinkedIn listing ≤ 7 days old **and** LinkedIn job ID ≥ 4466000000 (IDs are sequential; this drops reposts of old reqs).
2. Opened each posting: "Reposted" flag, applicant count, Easy Apply vs employer route.
3. Employer-side date where available (Workable/Ashby/Workday/Trackr opening date).
4. Body screen: any years-of-experience ≥1, no sponsorship / permanent-RTW, clearance/defence, PhD, fixed-term, actuarial/financial consulting, quant.

Sources: LinkedIn (55 queries × 9 UK locations, 7 days → 1,290 unique), Trackr (UK tech/finance/engineering grad trackers, opened ≥17 Sep), Glassdoor (no CLI; web search returned no Glassdoor pages, only recruiter reposts).
